Pair role-play · Your turn

Recruiter: Welcome! Could you introduce yourself and explain why you are interested in this IT position?

Candidate: Introduce yourself, explain your interests and continue answering at least six interview questions.

Follow-up questions: Can you give me an example? What would you do in that situation? How would you help the team?

Closing: Recruiter: “Do you have any questions for us?” Candidate: Ask one professional question about training, team projects or responsibilities.
